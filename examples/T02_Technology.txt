Technology Development Agreement (fictional test)
Client: Cloudgum Studio Pty Ltd. Developer: Blue Wattle Labs Pty Ltd.
1 Governing law: Victoria, Australia. Courts in Victoria have non-exclusive jurisdiction.
2 Services: Developer will build a customer portal according to the attached specification.
3 Delivery: Three milestones. Dates and acceptance criteria are to be agreed later.
4 Fees: AUD 40000 plus applicable GST. Client pays the entire fee before development starts.
5 Intellectual property: All project intellectual property, including bespoke deliverables, remains owned by Developer. Client receives a revocable licence during the term only.
6 Acceptance: Deliverables are deemed accepted immediately upon delivery, whether or not tested.
7 Liability: Client indemnifies Developer for all losses of any kind. Developer has no liability under this agreement.
8 Termination: Developer may terminate for convenience on one day's notice. No refund is payable. Client must pay all remaining fees.
9 Confidentiality: Each party must protect the other's confidential information for two years, subject to lawful disclosure requirements.
