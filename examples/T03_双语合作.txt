合作协议 / Cooperation Agreement (fictional)
甲方 / Party A: 云桥软件公司。乙方 / Party B: 海蓝设计公司。
1 适用法律：本协议适用英格兰与威尔士法律。Governing law: England and Wales.
2 双方共同开发并销售一款设计软件。The parties will jointly develop and market a design application.
3 合作收入由双方各分配50%，费用承担方式待定。Revenue is shared equally; allocation of expenses is to be agreed.
4 保密义务在协议终止后持续两年。Confidentiality obligations continue for five years after termination.
5 知识产权由双方共同拥有，许可机制另行约定。The parties jointly own project IP, with licensing arrangements to be agreed.
6 本合同英文版本优先。中文版本优先适用。The English version prevails.
7 各方承担依法应由其承担的税费，发票安排另行商定。Each party bears its applicable taxes; invoicing arrangements are to be agreed.
